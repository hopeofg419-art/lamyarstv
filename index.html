📺 Smart TV Installation Guide

For Samsung, LG, and other Smart TVs that don't support direct APK installs, follow these steps:

  • Download and install the "Downloader" app from your TV's app store.
  • Open Downloader and enter the Lamyar Elite Player download link (shown after purchase, or available on request).
  • Allow installation from unknown sources when prompted.
  • Once installed, open the app — your unique Device ID will appear on the welcome screen.
  • Head to the Activation page to enter your Device ID and choose a plan.

Is sideloading safe? +
Yes — sideloading simply means installing an app from outside the default app store. It's a normal, standard process on Android and FireStick devices when the source is trusted.
The app won't install — what do I do? +
Make sure "Install from unknown sources" is enabled in your device settings, and that you have enough free storage. If it still fails, reach out on the Support page.
